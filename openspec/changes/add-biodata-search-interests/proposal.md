## Why
Members need to find suitable rishta profiles and send/receive interest requests safely (contact hidden until mutual accept).

## What Changes
- Biodata search screen with filters, chips, match %, shortlist.
- Interest flow: send, accept, reject, withdraw, expire after 7 days, daily limit, admin help request.
- Area alert helper `NewBiodataAlert` for admin approval hook.
- Demo seeder + factories for testing.

## Out of scope
Blocking users, sub-admin areas, OTP.
