SamajBook - images the owner can drop in (no code change needed)

1) Samaj logo
   public/images/samaj-logo.svg   (preferred)   or   public/images/samaj-logo.png
   -> automatically replaces the built-in marigold/heart emblem everywhere
      (header, footer, login/register, status page). Use a transparent
      background and a roughly square or wide emblem (height ~ 128px+ for PNG).

2) Sant Gadge Maharaj photograph (portrait, shown in an arched frame)
   public/images/sant-gadge-maharaj.jpg   (.webp or .png also work)
   -> shown on the landing page in "समाज की विरासत". Use a portrait photo,
      about 4:5 ratio (e.g. 800 x 1000 px), under 300 KB. Use only a photo you
      have the right to publish (public-domain or with samaj permission).
   Until the file exists, a respectful illustrated placeholder is shown.

3) Quote wording
   The "संत गाडगे महाराज का संदेश" line is the $gadgeQuote variable at the top of
   resources/views/landing/index.blade.php. Please verify the exact wording
   with the samaj elders before launch.
